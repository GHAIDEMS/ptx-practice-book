Theorem 3.3.1 Infinitely Many Primes.

There are infinitely many prime numbers.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Suppose the primes were \(p_1, \dots, p_r\text{.}\) Let \(N = p_1 p_2 \cdots p_r + 1\text{.}\) No \(p_i\) divides \(N\text{,}\) since each leaves remainder \(1\text{.}\) But \(N \gt 1\) has a prime divisor, a contradiction.
