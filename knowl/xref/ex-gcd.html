Example 3.2.1 A Greatest Common Divisor.

Find the greatest common divisor of \(48\) and \(18\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
Repeated division gives
🔗
\begin{align*} 48 \amp= 2 \cdot 18 + 12\\ 18 \amp= 1 \cdot 12 + 6\\ 12 \amp= 2 \cdot 6 + 0\text{.} \end{align*}
The last nonzero remainder is \(6\text{,}\) so \(\gcd(48,18) = 6\text{.}\)
